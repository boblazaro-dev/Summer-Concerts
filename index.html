Boogie in the Park

Kure Beach

📍 Ocean Front Park & Pavilion 🗓️ 1st & 3rd Sunday · May–Oct 🕔 5:00–7:00 PM
Get directions Check the forecast Restaurants nearby Official schedule ↗

Good to know

Skips the weekends of July 4th and Labor Day. Genres rotate week to week — funk, country, reggae, soul, and more.

While you're there

Turtle Talk runs Monday evenings and the Kure Beach Market pops up Tuesday mornings, both nearby at Ocean Front Park.

Holden Beach Summer Concert Series

Holden Beach

📍 Bridgeview Park, near Town Hall (moves to Block Q mid-season) 🗓️ Sundays · May 24–Sep 6 🕡 6:30–8:00 PM
Get directions Check the forecast Restaurants nearby Official schedule ↗

Good to know

Genres run from beach music to jazz, oldies, and rock 'n' roll. Midway through the season the venue is scheduled to shift from Bridgeview Park to Block Q — check the town's page before you head out.

Questions?

Call the Town of Holden Beach at (910) 842-6488 for the latest details.

Sunset Sound Waves

Sunset Beach

📍 Town Park, 206 Sunset Blvd. North 🗓️ Wednesdays (+ Fri Jul 3) · May 27–Sep 2 🕕 6:00–8:00 PM
Get directions Check the forecast Restaurants nearby Official schedule ↗

Good to know

Sixteen dates of free live music — beach music, classic rock, and variety acts — organized and funded by the Town of Sunset Beach, plus a bonus Friday show on July 3.

Rain plan

No formal rain policy is published; cancellations or changes are posted on the town's website and social media, so check before you leave.

Live in Leland

Leland

📍 Founders Park Amphitheater, 113 Town Hall Dr. 🗓️ Select dates · Jul–Sep 🕕 6:00–8:00 PM
Get directions Check the forecast Restaurants nearby Official schedule ↗

Good to know

Leland's newest series, launched with the Founders Park grand opening in 2026. Expect a mix of live music and theatrical performances, plus food trucks, a beer garden, and yard games.

Parking

Limited lot at the park; overflow parking is available at First Baptist Church (517 Village Road) and North Brunswick High School.
